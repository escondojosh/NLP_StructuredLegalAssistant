EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Bridging Program for the Filipino Family or “Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino
Program", also known as 4Ps is a conditional cash transfer program under the
Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), aims to eradicate extreme
intergenerational poverty cycle that evolves to health, nutrition and education to the
classified as the poorest among the poor households with children ranging from 18 years
old and below in response to Sustainable Development Goals of the national government
for a maximum of seven (7) years.

The program has focused on Sustainability of Social Assistance and Development
that invests in human capability-building with cash provision assistance to help alleviate
poverty. The Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) has issued the
Pantawid Pamilya Cash Card to qualified beneficiaries as a medium to extend
government assistance through the “Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps)”. The
program targets at improving the state of education, health and nutrition of poor
households, those with children aged 0-18 years old.

The City of Manila has a total of 34,799 (from 3,621,833) Registered and Active
Beneficiaries in the National Capital Region (NCR) as of December 31, 2024. Out of the
34, 799 beneficiaries, recorded and identified composition of 242 have been engaged in
cash card pawning except for the unreported or unverified.

The acts of pawning 4Ps cash cards only sabotage and weaken the purpose and
objective, leaving the beneficiaries burdensome facing huge challenges to improve
financial breakthrough.

The Local Advisory Council (LAC) initially conducted an assessment and
verification process such as validating and confirming reports in pawning cases as these
may lead to further steps, establishing basis to create appropriate and applicable course
of actions that address possible reasons and cause of doing such act by the beneficiaries
and providing other means and/or alternative solutions of preventive measures for its
discontinued practice.

The DSWD through the National Advisory Council (NAC) Resolution No. 20, Series
of 2014, has conveyed clearly the implementing procedures and guidelines in cases of
Cash Card Pawning wrongdoings with corresponding appropriate sanctions based on the
merit and gravity of the case.

The Ordinance is necessary to determine and prevent the recurrence of 4Ps Cash
Card Pawning incidents. With its passing and approval, the target to expose the root
cause that greatly contributes to the incidents may be prevented, lessen and resolved to
non-existence in the future. This will also help to enforce sanctions and penaities to
whoever will tolerate and engage of this malpractice.

The relevance and essence to institutionalize a Task Force to address issues and
to put an end to the negative effects of the illegal pawning activities being unable to
access their grants and falling into debt traps due to high loan interest rates.

Be it ordained by the City Council of Manila, in session assembled, THAT:

SECTION 1. Title. — This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
PROHIBITING PRIVATE INDIVIDUALS, GOVERNMENT OFFICIALS AND WORKERS,
GROUP OR BUSINESS ENTITIES, LOAN SHARK OPERATORS AND HOUSEHOLD
BENEFICIARIES OF PANTAWID PAMILYANG PILIPINO IN USING OR ACCEPTING
ISSUED 4PS CASH CARD AS COLLATERAL FOR LOAN, PROVIDING PENALTY FOR
VIOLATION THEREOF".

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby the policy of the City of Manila to
protect and uphold the rights and best interests of its citizens including the vulnerability
of the underprivileged from the mischievous individuals who are devouring and taking
advantage of the unfortunate situation of the indigent beneficiaries of our government's
“Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps)” by means of a loan grant cash cards serves
as an assurance or collateral. It is also the policy of the City to protect its inhabitants
nevertheless, help the national government in annihilating undesirable household
beneficiaries from the program.

SEC. 3. Objectives. — This Ordinance aspires to reach and achieve the following
objectives:

1. To resolve the misapplication and abuse of Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino
Program (4Ps) cash cards in the City of Manila;

2. To penalize 4Ps beneficiaries in the City who shall pawn their cash cards
for a loan to any lenders may it be agencies, institutions or private money
lender individuals; and

3. To penalize government officials and workers, private groups or business
entities and other form of loan shark operators who are accepting or
requiring 4Ps Cash Cards as collateral.

SEC. 4. Definition of Terms. —

a. 4Ps - also known as "Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program" or "The
Bridging Program” is a conditional cash transfer program of the national
government under the Department of Social Welfare and Development
(DSWD) that aims to eradicate extreme intergenerational poverty cycle that
evolves to health, nutrition and education to the classified as the poorest
among the poor households with children ranging from 18 years old and
below in response to Sustainable Development Goals of the national
government.

b. Beneficiaries - refers to active households or families within the city duly
registered in “Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program’.

c. Business Entities - various establishments such as supermarkets,
cooperatives, stores, banks, lending institutions and other same business
premises.

d. Cash Card - electronic payment automated teller machine card issued by
the government used to extend financial assistance to 4Ps beneficiaries.

e. Cash Card Pawning - an act of giving the 4Ps Cash Card to a loan shark,
private individuals, government officials and workers, group or business
entities as collateral or security for credit, money borrowed or loan.

f. Collateral - any security provided by a borrower to guarantee repayment of
loan/debt as pertaining to the 4Ps Cash Cards.

g. Conditional Cash Transfer - is a monetary grant released to 4Ps
beneficiaries by the national government thru DSWD with a condition of
compliance to the monthly obligation set by the program.

h. DSWD - Department of Social Welfare and Development is the executive
department of the government responsible for the protection of the social
welfare of rights of Filipino and to promote social development that aims to
alleviate and diminish hunger and poverty, equal access to opportunities,
enabled by a fair, just and peaceful society.

i. Government Officials and Workers - an individual working in public office
(managerial or rank-in-file) and/or any individual elected or appointed in
public office with any rank and position.

j. Grievance Redness System - grievance committee system that receives
complaints on Cash Card Pawnings, use for gambling, loan collateral,
repayment or any form of misrepresentation.

k. Group - any association, organization or number of persons considered as
collective unit.

|. Intergenerational Poverty Cycle - IGP described as poverty persists from one
generation to next or cycle of poverty that repeats from generation to
generation.

m. LAC - Local Advisory Council is a body wherein strategies on poverty
alleviation, social welfare, development and protection are being monitored
and discussed.

n. Loan Shark - is a person or entity that offers loan with high interest rates,
exceeding legal limits and typically engages in predatory lending practices.
Borrowers who resort to loan sharks often find themselves trapped in a cycle
of debt. Operating outside bonds of law with intimidation, force repayment
and threats.

o. NAC - National Advisory Committee is the highest policy-making inter-
agency body that provides policy directions and guidance on matters
pertaining to "Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program” implementation.

p. NAC 20 - National Advisory Committee of 2014 - procedures and guidelines
on misdemeanor cases to the approved policy misbehavior of the
beneficiaries, as stipulated on NAC 2 and 10.

q. NCR - National Capital Region, also known as the capital region of the
Philippines.

r. Non- 4Ps Beneficiary - Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino non-member.

s. Poorest Among the Poor - an individual, who lived below the poverty
threshold, has very limited or none access to sufficiently meet basic needs
of the society.

t. Poverty Alleviation Strategy - National poverty reduction strategy thru cash
provision assistance for the sustainability of social assistance and social
development that enable 4Ps households with children ranging from zero to
eighteen (0-18) years old investing in human capability-building that
improves the state of education, health and nutrition.

u. Private Individual - a person who is not a government employee or official
and not a beneficiary of a particular program.

SEC. 5. Prohibited Acts. —

1. It shall be unlawful for any 4Ps household beneficiaries or any member of
the families to use and engage in Cash Card Pawning as stipulated in NAC
Resolution No. 20 of the “Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps)”.

2. It shall be considered unlawful for any individual, group or business
establishments, loan shark operators to use or accept 4Ps Cash Cards as
loan collaterals.

3. Pawning of 4Ps Cash Cards used as collateral for loan procurement,
repayment, settlement of financial obligations, gambling or barter in
exchange of any illegal substances, liquor and other services.

SEC. 6. Coverage. —

1. 4Ps Household Beneficiaries and its relatives up to the third (3d) civil degree
of consanguinity of affinity and/or a representative equipped with duly
notarized SPA (Special Power of Attorney) who may be holding the Cash
Card in behalf of the beneficiary.

2. 4Ps non-beneficiaries consisting of Private Individuals, Group or Business
Entities, Loan Shark Operators, Associations, Cooperatives and the like
requiring Cash Cards as collateral for loan.

SEC. 7. Penalty Clause. — For “Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program”
beneficiaries found violating this ordinance, shall be penalized as follows:

1. For Household Beneficiaries and the same —

a. First Offense - Counseling by the City Link with written warning from the
Head of the City Operations Office and blocking of Cash Card Account
and counseling of Social Worker from the City Social Welfare and
Development Office;

b. Second Offense - Counseling by the Social Welfare Officer from the
DSWD-NCR and City Social Welfare and Development Office, a written
warning from the Regional Director while the grant of the inappropriate
households shall be suspended / will not be released for one (1) payment
or for period of two (2) months and a fine of Three Thousand Pesos
(PhP3,000); and

c. Third Offense - Non-appealable delisting of the inappropriate household
under the Grievance Redness System (GRS) Fraud duly verified and
approved by the Regional Director and tagging to be facilitated by the
Regional Grievance Coordinators.

2. For “Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program” Non-Beneficiaries Lenders -
private individuals, government officials and workers, group or business
entities, loan shark operators, collectors, money lenders or financiers found
violating this Ordinance, shall be penalized as follows:

a. First Offense - Written Notice shall be issued by the Local Advisory
Council (LAC) to the private individual, group or business entities, loan
shark operators, collectors, money fenders or financiers duly
acknowledged by the Pawner and Pawnee and Two Thousand Pesos
(PhP2,000.00) fine for every Cash Card they are holding and/or
imprisonment of two (2) months upon the discretion of the court that shall
be imposed for the violation of this Ordinance payable to the City
Treasurer's Office and/or suspension of business permit. However, a
written endorsement to the Head Office by the LAC (if government
official/employee) with attachment to individual service record shall be
made.

b. Second Offense - A fine of Three Thousand Pesos (PhP3,000.00) and/or
imprisonment of five (5) months upon the discretion of the court that shall
be imposed for the violation of this Ordinance payable to the City
Treasurer's Office and/or suspension of business permit. However, a
written endorsement to the Head Office by the LAC (if government
Official/employee) with attachment to individual service record shall be
made.

SEC. 10. Disposition of Fines. — All fines collected in the enforcement of this
Ordinance shall be deposited into a Trust Fund of the respective Local Government Unit/s
(LGUs). Said funds shall be allocated for the implementation of programs and activities
related to “Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps)’, subject to standard auditing and
accounting rules and procedures.

SEC. 11. Separability Clause. — In the event that any provision of this Ordinance
is deemed unconstitutional and/or illegal by competent authority, such a declaration shall
not impair the validity or enforceability of the remaining provisions herein. Consequently,
the unaffected provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 12. Repealing Clause. — Any ordinance found to be inconsistent with the

provisions of this ordinance is hereby repealed, modified, or amended accordingly to
ensure alignment and compliance with the current legislative framework.
